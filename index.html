Find your way

Touch

Left thumb to move. Hold the sword to attack.
Use the boot to dash and the hand to interact.

Controller

Left stick to move. A / Cross to attack.
B / Circle to dash. X / Square to interact.

Keyboard

WASD or arrows to move. Space to attack.
Shift to dash. E to interact. Escape to pause.

Defeat enemies to open the way down. Collect coins for the merchant after every three combat floors.
